Questions students ask.

Do I need a school account?

No. Sign up with your phone number or email. If your school already uses SchoolFlow, you can also log in with your school username and school code.

How do I pay for the weekly pass?

Send the amount by bank transfer, then enter your transfer reference in the app. Your pass starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Can I practise for a job interview?

Yes. Choose Mock interview, pick the type (job, banking, civil service, visa and more), tell us the role, and answer the questions one by one. Each answer gets a score, feedback and a model answer. Each answer you submit for feedback uses one free question.

Are the questions the real exam questions?

No. The AI writes new questions in the style of each exam. They are for practice, so always check important facts with your textbook or teacher.

What happens to my progress if the pass ends?

Nothing is lost. Your XP, streak, badges and history stay. You only need a new pass to start new quizzes.

Can I do 40 questions at once?

Yes, with an active pass. With free questions you can take quizzes up to the number you have left.
